Instructions

Welcome to the Virtual Piano! Use your QWERTY keyboard to play notes. The piano spans 36 keys from C3 to C6.

Keyboard Mapping

Each piano key corresponds to a key on your QWERTY keyboard. The mapping is designed to match the physical keyboard layout for easier play.

Tips

  • Press the corresponding QWERTY key to play a note.
  • Use multiple keys simultaneously to play chords.
  • Clicking on the piano keys with your mouse will also play the notes.
